How to read this. Two diseases are neighbours when their causal genes share a specific Reactome pathway and their symptom profiles overlap; mechanism and phenotype similarity are scored separately. Observed links come straight from a database or a verified web page, extracted links were read from a paper abstract by an OpenAI model with the quote checked word for word, and inferred links are proposals computed from the graph. Patient groups, studies, researchers and paper evidence are gathered for the STXBP1 slice ( diseases) so far; elsewhere the atlas shows biology only. Researchers are matched across sources by name and institution, which is not verified. For research use only, not medical advice.
