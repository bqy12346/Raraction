Demo graph for ten epilepsy and synaptic genes

What is and is not in this graph. Every link comes from a structured database (HPO, Orphanet, OMIM through HPO, Reactome), so all are “observed”. Only three gene-to-disease links state a direction, all gain of function, drawn as thick labelled lines. Links extracted from papers by a language model would appear as dashed lines; none exist yet. Symptoms are limited to the 30 most specific ones shared by at least two diseases. For research use only, not medical advice.
